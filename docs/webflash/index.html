RemoteStart — flash a new unit

Flashes the latest built firmware onto a blank (or previously different) board straight from your browser — no ESP-IDF, no esptool install. Works in Chrome or Edge on desktop (Web Serial), over a USB cable. Pick the card for the unit you are building.

Choose the right unit. MasterRemote and SlaveHonda are both plain ESP32 boards, so the flasher cannot tell them apart — whichever button you press is what the board becomes. (SlaveWallas and ESPNowRelay need an ESP32-C6; the flasher refuses them on any other chip.)

Before you start
  1. Plug the board into this computer via USB. ESP32 boards (MasterRemote, SlaveHonda) use a CP210x USB-serial chip — install the Silicon Labs driver if no port shows up. ESP32-C6 boards use their native USB port; if it doesn't appear, hold BOOT while plugging in.
  2. Click the unit's Flash button and pick its serial port from the browser prompt.
  3. Choose Erase device when offered. A board with unknown earlier firmware can keep stale data outside the areas being written, which has left a radio silent before — a clean install avoids that.
  4. After flashing, the unit reboots into its own WiFi access point, <Unit>-Config (password honda1234). Join it and open http://192.168.4.1/wifi-setup in a normal browser tab. Scan, pick your WiFi, enter the password and press Connect & Save.
  5. The unit restarts on your WiFi. Let this phone or PC rejoin your home WiFi and keep the page open: it moves on by itself to the unit's own address — http://masterremote.local, slavehonda.local, slavewallas.local or espnowrelay.local. More in the README's "First Startup" section.

No pairing is needed: units find each other over ESP-NOW by role. Every update after this first flash goes over the air, from the unit's own OTA Update tab — this page is only needed once, for the initial flash.
